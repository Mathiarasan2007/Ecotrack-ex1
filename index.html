Public Education Portal

Know Where Your Waste Belongs.

Effective recycling starts at the kitchen table. Use the EcoTrack directory to understand bin color coding and proper disposal guidelines.

Quick test:
Green Bin

Wet Waste

Biodegradable organic matter suitable for municipal biomethanation and composting facilities.

  • Fruit & vegetable peels
  • Leftover food & cooked rice
  • Tea bags & coffee grounds
  • Garden leaves & small twigs
Blue Bin

Dry Waste

Non-biodegradable, non-recyclable or mixed municipal solid packaging items free from food residue.

  • Clean snack packaging wraps
  • Styrofoam food trays
  • Used paper napkins & tissues
  • Plastic stationery & pens
Teal Bin

Recyclable Waste

Commercial-grade raw recyclable commodities routed to registered formal material recovery plants.

  • PET water & soda bottles
  • Corrugated cardboard boxes
  • Aluminum soda & tin cans
  • Glass beverage jars & bottles
Red / Amber Bin

Special-Care Waste

Toxic, electronic, biomedical, or flammable materials requiring dedicated certified incineration handling.

  • Lithium & alkaline batteries
  • Broken CFL bulbs & mercury tubes
  • Expired medications & syringes
  • Discarded e-waste smartphones
